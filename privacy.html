01

What Information Do We Collect?

Account Information

When an organisation administrator creates a user account, we collect:

  • Display Name
  • Username
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number (optional)
  • Role (assigned by the administrator)

Usage and Technical Data

When you log in or use our Services, we automatically collect:

  • IP Address
  • User Agent (browser and device information)

Delivery Data

As a last mile courier platform, we collect the following recipient information to fulfil deliveries:

  • Recipient Name
  • Delivery Address
  • Postal Code
  • Phone Number

This information is provided by your organisation and is used solely to facilitate delivery operations.

Mobile App — Location Data

When you use our mobile application, we collect location data in both the foreground and background for the following purposes:

  • EPOD Location Capture — when an Electronic Proof of Delivery (EPOD) is taken, your device's location is captured and overlaid on the EPOD image to verify the delivery location.
  • Real-Time Driver Tracking — your location is tracked while the app is in use and while it is running in the background, to enable real-time visibility of driver whereabouts during active deliveries.
Location data is collected solely for delivery operations. It is not used for advertising or shared with third parties beyond what is necessary to operate these features. You will be prompted to grant location permissions when you first use the app. Background location access is required for driver tracking to function correctly.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use authentication cookies to maintain your session and keep you securely logged in. These cookies are essential for the operation of the platform and are not used for advertising or tracking.

We also use Cloudflare services to help protect and operate our platform. Cloudflare may process limited technical data as part of this. For more information, see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Account Management — to create, manage, and maintain user accounts on your organisation's behalf

  • Administrative Communications — to send notices about updates, changes to terms, or security alerts

With Your Consent

04

How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as necessary to provide our Services, and for up to 2 years thereafter, unless a longer period is required by law.

When personal information is no longer necessary, we will securely delete or anonymise it. If deletion is not immediately possible, we will isolate the data from further processing until deletion is possible.

05

How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures, including secure session management, platform protection services, and access logging for incident response.

However, no system is completely secure. While we strive to protect your data, you are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials and ensuring secure access to your account.

06

Do We Collect Information From Minors?

Our Services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we become aware that a minor has provided us with personal information, we will deactivate the account and delete such data promptly.

Please contact us at contact@betteragesolutions.com if you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a minor.

07

What Are Your Privacy Rights?

We operate in Singapore and comply with the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data
  • Withdraw consent for the collection, use, or disclosure of your personal data (subject to legal or contractual restrictions)

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at contact@betteragesolutions.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

For more information on your rights under the PDPA, visit the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC).
